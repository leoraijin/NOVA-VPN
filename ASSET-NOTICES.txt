NOVA VPN 2.1 — visual asset notes
=================================

Assets\nova-mark.png and derived Assets\nova-v2.ico
  Prompt summary: centered icon-only NOVA VPN brand mark combining an abstract
  N, shield and flowing connection path, violet-to-cyan frosted-glass geometry,
  transparent background, no text or watermark.

Assets\nova-backup.png
  Prompt summary: companion encrypted-backup icon, crystalline violet-to-cyan
  vault/archive capsule with a folded ribbon motif, transparent background,
  no text, padlock, document outline or watermark.

The .ico file is a deterministic format conversion of nova-mark.png for the
Windows executable. Vector interface icons are code-native WPF geometries and
were not raster-generated.

Offline server-region map data
==============================

NovaVpn/Assets/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson
NovaVpn/Assets/ne_50m_populated_places.geojson
  Source: Natural Earth, 1:110m Cultural Vectors (country boundaries) and
  1:50m Cultural Vectors (populated places),
  https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/110m-cultural-vectors/ and
  https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-cultural-vectors/
  The Natural Earth raster and vector datasets are in the public domain under
  the project's terms of use. The files are embedded in NOVA VPN so the map
  works offline. Country/city markers are profile-derived display hints, not
  live IP geolocation or exact VPN data-center coordinates.
